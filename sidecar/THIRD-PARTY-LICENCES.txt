Third-party components in the Kai voice engine
==============================================

This bundle is a frozen Python program. It redistributes the libraries below.
Each is used unmodified; their own licence texts ship inside the bundle, under
_internal/, in the metadata directories the packages carry.

  coqui-tts (a maintained fork of Coqui TTS)   Mozilla Public License 2.0
  PyTorch (torch, torchaudio, torchcodec)      BSD 3-Clause
  Transformers, tokenizers, huggingface-hub    Apache License 2.0
  librosa, scikit-learn, scipy, numpy, numba   BSD 3-Clause
  soundfile, soxr                              BSD 3-Clause / LGPL-2.1 (libsndfile)
  einops, inflect, num2words, pysbd, anyascii  MIT / Apache 2.0

MPL-2.0 (coqui-tts) requires that the source of covered files be made
available. It is unmodified and published by its authors at
https://github.com/idiap/coqui-ai-TTS — nothing in this bundle patches it.


What is NOT in this bundle
--------------------------

The XTTS-v2 model weights.

They are licensed under the Coqui Public Model License, which permits
non-commercial use only, and they are not redistributed here. The engine
downloads them from Coqui on first use, and refuses to do so until the person
using the app has accepted that licence in Kai's settings.

That distinction is the reason this bundle can be published at all: everything
inside it is under a licence that permits redistribution. The one component
that is not, is not included.
